# Corporate Credit Risk Analysis & AI-Assisted Decision Support
### Mahindra & Mahindra Limited | FY2024–FY2026

> **Disclaimer:** This project is for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes only. It does not constitute an official credit assessment, lending decision, credit rating, or investment advice. Financial figures are sourced from publicly available company disclosures and credit-rating publications. Refer to the Source Register & Verification Log for source details and verification notes.

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates my approach to analysing corporate financial performance, evaluating credit risk indicators, reconciling financial data, and presenting findings for decision support.

I analysed publicly available financial information relating to Mahindra & Mahindra Limited across FY2024, FY2025, and FY2026. The analysis focuses on revenue growth, profitability, borrowings, operating cash flow, liquidity considerations, and selected Financial Services asset-quality indicators.

I also used AI-assisted tools to support analysis and documentation, with the objective of improving efficiency while documenting assumptions, limitations, and areas requiring further verification.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse financial performance over three financial years.
- Evaluate profitability, borrowing trends, and operating cash flow.
- Examine selected liquidity and debt-maturity indicators.
- Review asset-quality indicators for the Financial Services business separately from consolidated group metrics.
- Identify data inconsistencies and document reconciliation requirements.
- Communicate findings through an Excel workbook, credit risk memo, and executive presentation.
- Document the methodology, limitations, sources, and use of AI-assisted tools.

## 3. Key Financial Findings

The following figures summarise the analysis. Amounts are in ₹ crore unless otherwise stated.

| Financial Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | 139,078.27 | 159,210.82 | 197,792.78 |
| Profit before tax | 15,977.79 | 19,079.62 | 25,184.76 |
| Consolidated profit | 12,269.82 | 14,073.17 | 18,621.71 |
| Borrowings, excluding leases | 103,203.23 | 121,172.95 | 129,614.50 |
| Cash flow from operations | -5,629.95 | -290.80 | 11,657.36 |

### Key Observations

**Revenue growth:** Revenue from operations increased by approximately 42.2% between FY2024 and FY2026, indicating substantial growth over the period.

**Profitability:** Profit before tax increased from ₹15,977.79 crore to ₹25,184.76 crore. The approximate PBT margin improved from 11.49% in FY2024 to 12.73% in FY2026.

**Operating cash flow:** Cash flow from operations improved significantly, moving from negative ₹5,629.95 crore in FY2024 to positive ₹11,657.36 crore in FY2026.

**Borrowings:** Borrowings increased over the three-year period, although annual growth slowed from approximately 17.4% in FY2025 to 7.0% in FY2026.

**Credit risk perspective:** Improving profitability and operating cash flow are positive indicators. However, borrowing levels, liquidity availability, debt maturities, and the sustainability of cash generation remain important areas for continued monitoring.

These observations are analytical findings, not a formal credit rating or lending recommendation.

## 7. Methodology

My approach followed these steps:

1. **Source collection:** Identify relevant company annual reports, credit-rating publications, and Financial Services disclosures.
2. **Data organisation:** Arrange financial figures by reporting period, metric, unit, and source.
3. **Financial analysis:** Calculate growth rates, profitability margins, cash-flow ratios, and borrowing indicators.
4. **Trend assessment:** Compare financial performance and selected risk indicators across three years.
5. **Data reconciliation:** Compare related figures and document differences requiring clarification.
6. **Risk interpretation:** Identify positive indicators, potential concerns, and areas for continued monitoring.
7. **Communication:** Present the results through a structured workbook, written memo, and executive presentation.
8. **Documentation:** Record assumptions, limitations, source references, and AI usage.

## 8. AI-Assisted Analysis

AI-assisted tools were used to support selected analytical and documentation tasks, such as organising observations, refining explanations, and structuring the presentation of findings.

AI-generated outputs should not be treated as independently verified financial evidence. Calculations, source references, interpretations, and reported figures require appropriate checks against original disclosures.

The methodology document explains the intended role of AI and the limitations of its use in this project.
