## 1. Project Overview

I developed this project to analyse the financial performance, debt profile, cash flow position, and selected credit risk indicators of Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (M&M) over FY2024–FY2026.

The project combines financial statement analysis, credit risk assessment, publicly available credit-rating information, and AI-assisted decision support. I present the analysis through an Excel workbook, a corporate credit risk memo, an executive presentation, and a methodology and AI usage document.

My objective was to build a structured analytical framework that identifies financial strengths, emerging risks, data inconsistencies, and areas requiring further investigation in a corporate credit review.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyse financial performance and profitability across three financial years.
- Evaluate borrowings, debt composition, and debt maturity considerations.
- Assess operating cash flow and its implications for debt servicing.
- Review selected asset quality indicators for the Financial Services business.
- Incorporate publicly available credit-rating information into the assessment.
- Identify discrepancies between reported financial figures and supporting schedules.
- Apply AI-assisted analysis while documenting its role and limitations.

## 3. Key Findings

The analysis highlights several important financial and credit considerations:

- **Revenue and profitability:** Consolidated revenue from operations and profit increased over FY2024–FY2026.
- **Borrowings:** Borrowings excluding lease liabilities increased during the analysis period, making debt composition and repayment requirements important areas of review.
- **Operating cash flow:** Cash flow from operations improved from negative levels in FY2024 and FY2025 to positive cash generation in FY2026.
- **Financial Services asset quality:** The reviewed Stage 3 indicators improved in FY2026 compared with FY2025.
- **Credit-rating context:** The assessment incorporates CRISIL's rating rationale dated 18 September 2026, including the published ratings for specified debt instruments and facilities.
- **Data reconciliation:** Differences between certain reported totals and supporting schedules are identified and documented rather than silently adjusted.

These findings form the basis of the project's credit risk discussion and areas for further review.

## 5. Analytical Methodology

I organised the assessment around the following areas:

### Financial Performance
- Revenue growth and profitability trends.
- Profit before tax and reported profit measures.
- Year-on-year financial comparisons.

### Debt and Borrowings
- Borrowings excluding lease liabilities.
- Current and non-current borrowing composition.
- Debt maturity considerations and reconciliation with reported borrowings.

### Cash Flow Analysis
- Trends in cash flow from operations.
- Operating cash flow relative to revenue.
- Cash generation and debt-servicing considerations.

### Financial Services Asset Quality
- Gross Stage 3 and net Stage 3 ratios.
- Stage 3 expected credit loss coverage.
- Separate treatment of Financial Services asset quality indicators and consolidated group-level financial figures.

### Credit-Rating Context
- Review of the published CRISIL rating rationale dated 18 September 2026.
- Consideration of published ratings alongside the financial analysis.

## 6. Data Quality and Limitations

Data reconciliation is an important part of this project.

The analysis identifies and documents:

- A difference between consolidated revenue from operations and the sum of reported segment external revenue.
- A difference between reported balance-sheet borrowings and the total in the debt maturity principal schedule.
- Potential restrictions or earmarking affecting the availability of certain other bank balances.
- Questions about the sustainability of the improvement in operating cash flow.
- Limitations associated with interpreting selected Financial Services asset quality indicators.
- Areas where additional source verification or calculation would be required.

These items are retained as open issues for further review. Reported figures are not silently adjusted to eliminate discrepancies, and the analysis does not assume that all reported bank balances are freely available to meet debt obligations.

Refer to the methodology document and source register for further details.
